Why ride it

Four days that cross most of Colorado's mountain country and never feel the same twice. Back roads and gold camps to the foot of Pikes Peak; petrified redwoods, South Park and Cottonwood Pass to Crested Butte; Kebler's aspens, the marble country, riverside hot springs and the remote Flat Tops to Steamboat; then Buffalo Pass, moose country in North Park, and down the Poudre to home. Three of the five passes are dirt.

  • ⛰Cottonwood, Kebler, Ripple Creek, Buffalo and Cameron passes
  • ♨Penny, Glenwood and Steamboat hot springs
  • 🦴Florissant's petrified redwoods
  • 🫎Moose country in North Park
  • 🏞The South Platte, the Crystal and the Poudre

Day 1

Boulder to Woodland Park

176 mi · about 4¾ h of riding · 40% dirt

🔍 Explore day 1 up close

Gold camps and back roads along the Front Range to the foot of Pikes Peak.

Morning

Boulder to Idaho Springs · 75 mi

mile 11● On the way

⛏Gold Hill

One of Colorado's first gold camps (1859): log cabins, a general store and dirt streets, high above Boulder.

mile 27● On the way

🧊Nederland

A funky mountain town at the foot of the Indian Peaks. For 30 years it kept a frozen man in a shed and held a festival for him (both have moved to Estes Park). Still here: the Carousel of Happiness, a 1910 carousel with new animals all carved by one man over 26 years.

mile 49● On the way

📸Panorama Point, Golden Gate Canyon

An overlook right on the way with about 100 miles of the Continental Divide spread out to the west. A state park pass may be needed to stop.

mile 65● On the way

👻Nevadaville

A near-empty gold camp: false fronts, mine dumps, and a Masonic lodge that still meets in its own ghost town.

mile 65● On the way

⛏Central City

The richest square mile on earth, the miners said: red brick, mine dumps and an 1878 opera house.

mile 75● On the wayCoffee

☕Idaho Springs

Coffee on Miner Street.

Midday

Idaho Springs to Evergreen · 19 mi

mile 94● On the wayLunch

🍴Evergreen

Lunch by Evergreen Lake, after the climb over Mestaa'ėhehe Pass (once Squaw Pass).

Afternoon

Evergreen to Woodland Park · 82 mi

mile 120● On the way

🏞The South Platte River

Foxton Road and the South Platte River Road follow the water down a deep granite canyon: easy riverside dirt and fly fishers in Gold Medal trout water.

mile 150● On the way

🌲Trail Creek and Ute Pass

Sandy forest roads through pink granite to Divide, then over Ute Pass to Woodland Park.

Tonight

Woodland Park

The City Above the Clouds, at 8,500 ft on the shoulder of Pikes Peak. Dinner in town, and the Rocky Mountain Dinosaur Resource Center if you arrive early.

Day 2

Woodland Park to Crested Butte

213 mi · about 4¾ h · a sixth dirt

🔍 Explore day 2 up close

Petrified redwoods, the Tarryall canyon, South Park, the Arkansas, and Cottonwood Pass to Crested Butte.

Morning

Woodland Park to Buena Vista · 128 mi

mile 16● On the way

🦴Florissant Fossil Beds

Giant petrified redwood stumps, 34 million years old, in a high meadow, right on the way. Coffee in Florissant just after.

mile 45● On the way

🐏Tarryall Road

A quiet road along Tarryall Creek between granite domes, where bighorn sheep sometimes stand on the road.

mile 85● On the way

🦌Across South Park

Past Como and across the huge high basin of South Park on Elkhorn Road to Hartsel: big sky, pronghorn and long dirt straights.

mile 113● On the way

⛰Trout Creek Pass

Out of South Park's south end and over an easy pass: the Collegiate Peaks fill the view ahead on the way down to the Arkansas valley.

mile 128● On the wayLunch

🍴Buena Vista

Lunch in a river town under the Collegiate Peaks.

Afternoon

Buena Vista to Crested Butte · 85 mi

mile 130● On the way

🚂The Midland tunnels

Just north of town an old railroad grade above the Arkansas runs through short rock tunnels, then back roads swing round to the foot of Cottonwood Pass.

mile 151● On the way

♨Cottonwood Hot Springs

Rustic pools in the pines right beside the road up the pass.

mile 165● On the way

⛰Cottonwood Pass

The Continental Divide at 12,126 ft, one of the highest roads in the state.

mile 180● On the way

🏞Taylor Park and Taylor Canyon

Down to a wide high park and its reservoir, then over the Jacks Cabin Cutoff to Crested Butte.

Tonight

Crested Butte

Painted false-front buildings on Elk Avenue, mountains on every side, and wildflowers everywhere in July. The best dinner town on the trip.

Day 3

Crested Butte to Steamboat Springs

257 mi · about 6 h · a quarter dirt

🔍 Explore day 3 up close

The long day: Kebler's aspens, the marble country and riverside hot springs, then the remote Flat Tops to Steamboat.

Morning

Crested Butte to Redstone · 58 mi

mile 7● On the way

🍂Kebler Pass

Gravel through one of the largest aspen groves in North America, under the jagged Ruby Range.

mile 49● On the way

⛰McClure Pass and the Crystal River

Over McClure Pass and down the Crystal River, with Hays Creek Falls right beside the road.

mile 51↗ Sidetrack · about 6 mi up the valley

🏛Marble

The quarry here supplied the marble for the Lincoln Memorial and the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier. The Crystal Mill, the most photographed mill in Colorado, is beyond it on a jeep road.

mile 58● On the wayCoffee

☕Redstone

A storybook company town of the 1900s: a row of old coke ovens and a castle built by a coal baron.

Midday

Redstone to Rifle · 55 mi

mile 61● On the way

♨Penny Hot Springs

Free natural hot pools at the edge of the Crystal River, right below the road.

mile 86● On the way

♨Glenwood Springs

Ride past what's billed as the world's largest hot springs pool, over 400 ft long, by the Colorado River.

mile 113● On the wayLunch

🍴Rifle

Lunch, or a little earlier in New Castle.

Afternoon

Rifle to Steamboat Springs · 144 mi

mile 154● On the way

⛽Meeker

Coffee and fuel in an old ranch town: the last fuel for about 80 miles. The free White River Museum fills log buildings from the 1880 army post, and Theodore Roosevelt stayed at the hotel on his hunting trips. Some of the hills on the way in burned in the 2025 Lee Fire.

mile 180● On the way

🌲The Flat Tops Trail

A byway up the White River to Ripple Creek Pass, with an overlook of the Flat Tops, huge lava-capped plateaus.

mile 188↗ Sidetrack · several miles off, in and out

🏞Trappers Lake

A lake under an amphitheater of lava cliffs. In 1919 Arthur Carhart was sent here to plan summer homes and argued for leaving it wild: many call it the birthplace of the wilderness idea. The day is already long, so only if you're ahead.

mile 218● On the way

⛰Dunckley Pass

The second gravel pass of the byway, 9,764 ft, with a pullout and the Flat Tops along the skyline.

mile 235● On the way

🏞Into Steamboat

Down the Yampa valley through old coal towns to Steamboat.

Tonight

Steamboat Springs

Ski Town USA, a real ranch town too, on the Yampa River. Soak at the hot springs in town, or up the hill at Strawberry Park's stone pools, then dinner on Lincoln Avenue.

Day 4

Steamboat Springs to Boulder

180 mi · about 4¼ h · a fifth dirt

🔍 Explore day 4 up close

Over Buffalo Pass to moose country, over Cameron Pass, and down the Poudre.

Morning

Steamboat to Walden · 47 mi

mile 1↗ Sidetrack · about 3 mi off

💧Fish Creek Falls

A 283-ft waterfall a short walk from the parking lot, just outside town.

mile 13● On the way

⛰Buffalo Pass

Dirt up and over the Park Range at the Continental Divide, then down through forest into North Park.

mile 47● On the wayLunch

🍴Walden

The Moose Viewing Capital of Colorado: keep an eye on the willows. An early lunch in a real ranch town.

Afternoon

Walden to Boulder · 133 mi

mile 71● On the way

🫎Moose Visitor Center

State Forest State Park's visitor center by the road at Gould, with a good chance of seeing the real thing in the willows nearby.

mile 79● On the way

⛰Cameron Pass

Under the Nokhu Crags at 10,276 ft, with more moose in the meadows.

mile 105● On the way

🏞The Poudre Canyon

Colorado's only Wild and Scenic river, and some 40 miles of canyon curves beside the water. The upper canyon burned in the 2020 Cameron Peak Fire and is still growing back.

mile 140● On the way

🌄Stove Prairie and Masonville

Out of the canyon over the Stove Prairie hills to Masonville, then home along the foothills below the red hogbacks.
